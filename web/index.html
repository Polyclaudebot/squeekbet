No contract is configured yet. Deploy SqueekBet on Sepolia with your wallet. You only need a little Sepolia ETH for gas. The deploying wallet becomes the only one that can open markets.

Why it's safe
  • Betting closes when the window starts, so nobody can bet after watching the outcome.
  • Results come from SqueekCore's settled onchain data. Nobody picks the winner.
  • Only the SqueekBet team can open markets, and that is all they can do. Nobody can withdraw the pooled SQK, cancel a market or pick a winner.
  • If nobody takes the other side, every bettor gets their stake back.
  • If settlement stalls or breaks for 24 hours after a market ends, refunds unlock.
  • You only approve the exact amount you bet, and every transaction is simulated first.
